About V2I

The vision behind India's premier civic intelligence platform.

Our Mission

V2I (Vote 2 India) was founded on the principle that a well-informed electorate is the cornerstone of a vibrant democracy. In a landscape often cluttered with fragmented information and biased narratives, we provide a unified, AI-driven platform that delivers clear, neutral, and accessible policy analysis to every Indian voter.

verified

Neutrality

We use advanced LLMs to analyze manifestos without political bias, focusing purely on policy intent.

translate

Accessibility

Breaking language barriers by providing real-time translations in 12+ regional Indian languages.

security

Privacy

Built with a privacy-first architecture, ensuring your personal identity and voting preferences remain anonymous.

Our Methodology

How we transform raw political data into actionable intelligence.

The V2I Analysis Pipeline

1
Data Ingestion
We crawl official party websites and ECI filings for the latest manifestos and policy documents.
2
AI Synthesis
Our Google Gemini-powered engine extracts key promises across 10 core sectors (Economy, Health, Education, etc.).
3
Alignment Scoring
Policies are cross-referenced with your anonymous preferences to generate an objective alignment score.

Security & Privacy

Your trust is our most valuable asset.

lock

Zero PII Retention

We do not store your Aadhaar or EPIC numbers. They are hashed locally and used only for one-time verification.

encrypted

End-to-End Encryption

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is protected by industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption.

visibility_off

Anonymous Analytics

Usage metrics are aggregated and anonymized to ensure individual voting patterns can never be reconstructed.

gavel

Regulatory Compliance

Fully compliant with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

V2I is designed to minimize data collection. We collect anonymous usage data and temporary session identifiers only.

2. How We Use Data

Data is used solely to provide personalized policy alignment scores and improve the accuracy of our AI synthesis engine.

3. Data Sovereignty

All user data resides on servers located within India, in compliance with national data residency requirements.
